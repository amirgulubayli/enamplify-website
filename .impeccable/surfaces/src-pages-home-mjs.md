Scope: enamplify.com marketing site, all page types; lead surface is the home page. Mode: Persuade (Insights articles and guides: Read).

Audience and job: COOs and Heads of Ops at 200–2,000 person organisations, London and Baku, reading on phones between meetings. Action: book a diagnostic call, when ready. Proof on hand: Google hackathon win, 10+ years, delivered-systems categories, grademy and pripitch. No client names, testimonials or real metrics; exhibits are labelled illustrative.

Constraints: zero-dependency static build; no stock photography; no hard-sell devices; sentence case, no em dashes, no decorative preheaders, no uppercase transforms.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Every outcome is shown as a numbered exhibit, the way a COO already reads a board pack. Refuses the category default of a dark gradient hero with a product screenshot and three feature cards, and refuses the cream-serif editorial look.

OWN-WORLD: White paper, deep navy ink, one electric-blue signal reserved for highlighted data, links and the primary button. Schibsted Grotesk display, Public Sans text, tabular figures. Hairline rules, exhibit labels, source lines, ledgers. One navy close band per page. Square corners, 1px rules, no shadows, no gradients.

STORY: The visitor sees the promise and an honest exhibit of where their team's week goes, recognises the situation, sees three outcomes they could take to the board, learns it starts small and safely, reads our commitments and proof, and books a call without being pushed.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left seven columns: H1 at display scale, lede, primary button "Book a diagnostic call" plus text link, one-line note. Right five columns: Exhibit 1, the stacked week bar with legend and source line. Header above with wordmark, four links, quiet button.

FORM: The Board Pack, position 1 on the grounded list (IMPECCABLE'S PICK over the rolled Annual Report), seed key ad3cf329. Code-led; no comp.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
