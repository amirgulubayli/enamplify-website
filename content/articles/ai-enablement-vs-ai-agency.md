Most leadership teams who want to do more with AI get two kinds of offer. One is an AI agency that will build something for them. The other is a training provider that will run a workshop. Both are useful in the right place. Neither usually changes how the organisation works a year later.

AI enablement is a third option, and it is the one Enamplify was built around.

## What an AI agency sells

An agency sells a finished system. You describe the problem, they build the solution, and you receive a tool.

That works well when the problem is stable and well understood. It works less well for the everyday work of an operations team, where the process changes every quarter and the people closest to it know things no brief captures. When the agency leaves, the knowledge of how the system works often leaves with them. The next change becomes a new project and a new invoice.

## What a workshop sells

A workshop sells awareness. People leave knowing more about what AI can do, and some of them try it.

The problem is what happens on Monday. Without an approved place to build, a method for choosing the right work and someone accountable for the result, enthusiasm fades or moves into personal accounts that leadership cannot see.

## What enablement leaves behind

AI enablement means your own people learn to build and run AI workflows on their real work, inside an environment your organisation controls. The consultancy’s job is to make that possible and then step back.

In practice, that is three things working together:

1. **A governed place to work.** People sign in with their company accounts, data stays where you decide, and every workflow has an owner and a review before it goes live.
2. **A method.** Teams learn how to pick work worth automating, write a clear brief, test the result and hand it over properly.
3. **Measured results.** Before anything starts, you agree what success looks like, so the outcome is something leadership can defend.

## Which one do you need?

If you have a single, well-defined problem and no appetite to own the solution, hire a builder. If you need people to understand what AI is, run a workshop. If you want AI to become part of how your team works, owned in-house and visible to the people accountable for it, look for enablement.
