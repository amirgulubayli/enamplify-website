There is a useful difference between being better prepared for a conversation and pretending to have had one.

AI can help organise information, draft a brief or turn notes into proposed next actions. Those uses can support a real person doing real work.

Fabricating familiarity, inventing an endorsement or implying personal attention that never occurred is a different choice. It changes what the recipient is being asked to believe.

For a client-facing business, that distinction should be part of the design.

## Support the person doing the work

Consider preparation. A structured summary of approved information can help someone identify what they know and what they still need to ask. The useful output is not a performance of intimacy. It is a clearer starting point for a genuine conversation.

The same is true afterwards. Drafting a follow-up from accurate notes can help a person keep commitments visible. They still need to check that the message reflects what was agreed and that they are willing to stand behind it.

The tool is supporting the relationship rather than impersonating its substance.

## Do not let fluency become a false claim

A draft may sound natural while containing an assumption about a person, a meeting or an organisation. Before sending it, check whether the apparent familiarity is supported.

Did someone actually say that? Was that promise made? Is the claimed example real? Does the writer have the authority to make the commitment?

These are editorial and professional questions, not just technical ones. A polished tone does not settle them.

## Define the boundaries before scaling

A team can agree simple standards: no invented customer quotes; no fictional personal experiences; no unsupported claims about a prospect; no automatic commitments without the appropriate approval.

Decide who reviews externally facing material and which kinds of messages require extra care. Make the route for correcting an error explicit.

The standards should apply whether a draft was written by a person, assisted by AI or assembled through a template. The relevant question is whether the communication is truthful, appropriate and owned by someone accountable.

## Keep real proof visible

For a business website, the same principle applies to testimonials, staff photography and case studies. An attractive page is not a reason to invent a result or imply a team that does not exist.

A founder-led practice can say it is founder-led. A product exploration can be labelled as a product exploration. A method can be shown through an honest sample rather than a fabricated customer dashboard.

Specific truth is a stronger foundation for the brand than simulated scale.

The point is not to avoid assistance. It is to use assistance in a way that respects the person on the other side of the communication.
